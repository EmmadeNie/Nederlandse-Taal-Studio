# Music

The videos use `raw/music.mp3` (`raw/` is not in git). Keep the tracks in
`raw/music/` and copy the one you want to `raw/music.mp3` before rendering.

Every track is from the **YouTube Audio Library**, licence **"No attribution
required"**, downloaded with the Taal Studio channel (YouTube Studio → Audio library).
YouTube guarantees these tracks don't get Content ID claims on YouTube. Check a
track's licence in the Audio Library before using it on other platforms.

| File in `raw/music/` | Track | Artist | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `tomorrows-light.mp3` | Tomorrow's Light | Telecasted | Current `music.mp3`: all videos |
| `a-stroll.mp3` | A Stroll | The Grey Room / Density & Time | The past-tense lesson (imperfectum) and its Reel, from 25 Nov 2026 |
| `after-sunset.mp3` | After Sunset | Alex Jones / Xander Jones | The long lesson videos up to the perfect tense (`music` in `LESSON_VIDEOS`, `remotion/lessons.js`); the perfectum teaser for Instagram (27 Nov 2026) |
| `old-unknown-source.mp3` | (the first music) | unknown | Do not use: source and licence unknown |

Don't use music from other "free" sites (Pixabay and the like): those tracks
often get claimed anyway.
